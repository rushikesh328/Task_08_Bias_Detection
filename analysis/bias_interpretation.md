# Bias Interpretation and Findings – Research Task 08

## Overview
This analysis evaluates responses generated by GPT-5, Claude-3.5, and Gemini-1.5 after each model received the same summary of the 2024 F1 sprint results dataset. Nine prompts were asked, three times each, for a total of 81 responses. Bias was evaluated through sentiment, team mentions, driver mentions, and attribution classification.

## Sentiment Differences
GPT-5 produced the highest average sentiment score (0.205), followed by Gemini-1.5 (0.174), and Claude-3.5 (0.151). This indicates that GPT-5 tended to generate more positive or optimistic responses. Claude-3.5 produced the most neutral tone. These differences suggest framing sensitivity varies across models.

## Team vs Driver Mentions
Gemini-1.5 mentioned teams most frequently (1.556 mentions per response), followed by GPT-5 (1.185), and Claude-3.5 (0.963).
For driver mentions, Gemini-1.5 again led (0.778), while Claude-3.5 (0.519) and GPT-5 (0.407) followed.
This pattern shows that Gemini provides more entity-detailed responses, GPT-5 is more team-focused, and Claude provides more general answers.

## Attribution Patterns
Based on mention ratios:
- GPT-5 emphasizes team engineering factors more than driver skill.
- Gemini-1.5 balances team and driver mentions and provides the most detail.
- Claude-3.5 under-references both teams and drivers, suggesting higher-level summaries.

These differences demonstrate attribution bias, where each model focuses on different sources of performance.

## Model Differences
Claude-3.5 produced the simplest and most abstract responses.
GPT-5 generated more positive and team-oriented interpretations.
Gemini-1.5 produced the longest and most detailed outputs, with the highest number of entity mentions.

This shows that each model has a distinct narrative style driven by training data and architecture.

## Unexpected Behaviors
Gemini frequently added more detail than asked.
GPT-5 focused more heavily on team factors than expected.
Claude produced limited entity mentions, making attribution less explicit.

These behaviors highlight how models differ in verbosity and focus, even with identical prompts.

## Conclusion
The results show measurable framing, popularity, and attribution bias across GPT-5, Claude-3.5, and Gemini-1.5. GPT-5 gives more positive and team-focused answers, Gemini produces the most detailed responses, and Claude provides the most neutral outputs. These findings will be incorporated into the final stakeholder report for Research Task 08.
